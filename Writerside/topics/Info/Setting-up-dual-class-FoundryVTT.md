# Setting-up-dual-class-(FoundryVTT)

> Already dual classed?
>> - A character made in the ComeOnOver PF2e Character Creator comes into Foundry with its dual class already set up.
>> - You only need this page for a sheet made some other way, like a blank sheet you filled in on Foundry.

> You do not need a GM.
>> - Tongs adds your second class for you, straight from your own Foundry screen.

> How to dual class.
>> - Go to [https://foundry.cooserver.duckdns.org/](https://foundry.cooserver.duckdns.org/), sign in with your ComeOnOver account and press Play.
>> - Find the Tongs tray.
>> - Press "Dual class…" (the C×2 button).
>> - Pick your sheet.
>> - Pick your 1st class, then your 2nd class. Starfinder classes are in the list too.
>> - Send it.
>> - Tongs builds the dual class and puts it on your sheet.
>> - If a class asks you to choose something (a skill, a school), choose it.

> Good to know.
>> - Kineticist has to be your 1st class. Inventor and Thaumaturge probably do too.
>> - Do not use the old "Based Dual Class" macro. Tongs replaces it.
>> - A sheet can only have one dual class. If yours needs changing, ask a GM.
>> - Your class feats can come from either of your classes.

> Something went wrong?
>> - Ask in your Discord channel or on the Telegram chat and a GM will help.
