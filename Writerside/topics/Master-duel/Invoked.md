# Invoked

Budget Invoked (no Aleister the Reminiscent). Card details: [Cards-Invoked](https://github.com/LewisIsWorking/PathWarsWiki/blob/main/Writerside/topics/Master-duel/Card-database/Cards-Invoked.md).

Starters: 3 Aleister, 3 Magical Meltdown, 1 Aiwass, 1 Sacred Spirit Sword Aiwass (about 69% to open one going first). Invoked was Tier 2 (power 10.0) on Master Duel Meta in Nov 2026 and untouched by the 5 Nov banlist.

## Line B: Aleister + a FIRE or WIND monster (Ash, Droll)

Source: Moha-MD, [Invoked | Easy Guide & Decklist](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c-exb_WP9fY); same as Taffy's spreadsheet line.

1. Normal Summon Aleister, search Invocation.
2. Link Aleister into Artemis.
3. Invocation: Aleister from the GY + the FIRE/WIND monster from hand → Sorath.
4. Invocation in the GY: shuffle it back, add Aleister.
5. Discard Aleister from hand targeting Sorath; Sorath revives Aleister.
6. Link Aleister + Artemis into Endymion; equip Artemis to it.
7. Artemis searches Crowley; Crowley summons itself.
8. Crowley fuses itself + Endymion → Baybarron; search Virakam.
9. Special Summon Virakam; set Sword.
10. Link Sorath + Baybarron into Invoker of Madness.
11. Sorath in the GY: Sorath + Aleister → Mechaba. Madness searches Invocation.
12. Invocation: Madness + Crowley from the GY → Oceanus (Extra Monster Zone).

End board: Oceanus, Mechaba, Virakam, set Sword.

## Line A: Aleister + any card (needs Almiraj)

Source: Taffy's [Invoked combo spreadsheet](https://www.masterduelmeta.com/spreadsheet/6ac95807501c79d600eec076) and Moha-MD.

1. Normal Summon Aleister, search Invocation.
2. Link Aleister into Almiraj.
3. Invocation: Aleister + Almiraj → Sorath.
4. Invocation in the GY: add the banished Aleister.
5. Discard Aleister targeting Sorath; Sorath revives Aleister.
6. Link Aleister + Sorath into Invoker of Madness.
7. Sorath in the GY: Sorath + Aleister → Baybarron.
8. Madness discards a spare card for Invocation; Baybarron searches Virakam.
9. Special Summon Virakam; set Sword.
10. Link Baybarron + Virakam into S:P Little Knight (banish a card; no direct attacks this turn).
11. Invocation: Madness + Virakam from the GY → Mechaba (Moha makes Elysium here instead).

End board: Little Knight, Mechaba, set Sword. If a fusion gets negated, the second Invocation can use Aleister from the GY to restart at step 3.

## Tips

- Oceanus in the **Extra Monster Zone** banishes everything sent to their GY; in a Main Monster Zone it forces attacks onto itself.
- Sword is a Quick-Play: it can't be used the turn it's set. On their turn it fuses with your banished monsters (e.g. Aleister + Madness → Caliga, or Oceanus).
- Little Knight's banish stops direct attacks that turn.
- Counters to expect: Ash on Baybarron's search, Imperm, Dominus Spark on Sorath, anything stopping banishing.

## Vs Natasha (Dragoon Horus)

- Go first when you can choose: Oceanus in the Extra Monster Zone kills Sarcophagus's GY engine.
- Side in 2 Super Polymerization and 3 Forbidden Droplet. Super Poly can't be responded to, and every monster she plays matches an Invoked fusion: DARK (Dragoon, Imsety) → Oceanus/Caliga, WATER (Duamutef) → Oceanus, EARTH (Qebehsenuef) → Baybarron, WIND (Hapi) → Raidjin/Sorath, Dragoon as Extra Deck monster → Omega.
- Negate before removing: Horus monsters trigger when her other cards leave the field. Droplet first, then banish King's Sarcophagus with Aeon.
- A Gaze of Timaeus Dragoon is banished in the End Phase of the next turn. Ghost Belle stops Gaze when Dark Magician is in her GY.

## Vs Jack (Cyber Dragon)

- Chimeratech Fortress takes your Machines (Mechaba); Megafleet takes monsters in the Extra Monster Zones. Neither is an effect, so stop the Cyber Dragon instead.
- Cyber Dragon Infinity steals Attack Position monsters: keep non-attackers in Defence.
