# Speed sheet

Pre-decided answers for live games, so nobody has to think while the timer runs. Card text for every card named here was checked against YGOPRODeck on 21 Nov 2026. Lines marked **UNTESTED** are built from card text, not from a guide.

See also: [Tournament decks](Tournament-decks.md), [Witchcrafter](Witchcrafter.md), [Invoked practice sheet](Invoked-practice.md), [Natasha](Natasha.md), [Luke](Luke.md).

## Fast mode

- **Under 60 seconds on the timer, or "FAST":** Claude replies with one line: the next action. No lookups, no explanations.
- **Under 20 seconds:** pass, or take the default move for that board below.
- **Before your turn starts** (while they're still playing), send the screenshot. The plan gets made on their time, not yours.
- **A small finished board beats a big unfinished one.** If the timer is under 60 seconds, stop extending and set what you have.

## Five rules for every game

1. **Lead with the search you can afford to lose.** Spell Power Mastery or Creation before Walpurgis; Meltdown before Aleister. The first search is the one that gets Ashed, Purged or Drolled.
2. **Read every face-up card of theirs before you commit.** Long-press it. True Light (Blue-Eyes) was a one-card board wipe we didn't use.
3. **Count lethal before the Battle Phase.** Their LP minus your attackers.
4. **Monsters you might attack with go in Attack Position.** You can't change position the turn they're summoned.
5. **Negate first, remove second** against boards that punish removal (Natasha's Horus, Rhino's Neos Fusion).
