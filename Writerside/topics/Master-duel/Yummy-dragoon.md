## How the deck works

- **Yummy engine.** Normal Summon any Yummy → Link it into **Yummy★Snatchy** → Snatchy places **Mignon** from the Deck → Mignon revives a Level 1 Yummy → Snatchy (counts as a Level 1 Tuner) + that Yummy → **Cupsy★Yummy Way**, which adds **2 Yummy monsters** and then you discard 1.
- **Small Yummies summon themselves from hand** while you control a Link-1 or a Level 2 Synchro (Marshmao: while all your monsters are LIGHT Beasts). These summons don't start a chain.
- **Herald of the Arc Light → Illusion of Chaos → Pharaoh's Servant.** When Herald goes to the GY it searches Illusion of Chaos. Illusion searches the Servant. The Servant summons itself by revealing a Spell and **Sets The Gaze of Timaeus** from the Deck.
- **Dark Magician of Destruction (DMoD)** comes out of the Extra Deck by banishing a Level 6+ DARK Spellcaster (Servant or Mind on Air), on a turn a Spell was activated. It searches a card that mentions Dark Magician and counts as Dark Magician.
- **Gaze of Timaeus** turns a "Dark Magician" (Servant or DMoD) on your field or in your GY into **Red-Eyes Dark Dragoon**. The Dragoon is banished in the End Phase of the next turn. **Set Gaze and use it on her turn**: then Dragoon lasts through her turn and yours.

## Line 1: Any Yummy + 1 discard

From sam95col's MDM combo. Swaps: **Borreload** instead of Zalen to send Herald to the GY; **Lollipo★Yummy Way + Baronne** instead of the Elfnote steps. UNTESTED.

**Needs:** any Yummy monster (Marshmao in the example; Cooky, Lollipo or Cupsy work the same) + 1 card to discard.

**Get Cupsy★Yummy Way**
1. Normal Summon **Marshmao☆Yummy**.
2. Link Marshmao → **Yummy★Snatchy**: place **Yummyusment☆Mignon** from the Deck.
3. **Mignon:** Special Summon Marshmao from the GY.
4. Synchro: Snatchy (as a Level 1 Tuner) + Marshmao → **Cupsy★Yummy Way**: add **Cupsy☆Yummy** + **Cooky☆Yummy**, discard the spare.
5. Special Summon **Cupsy☆Yummy** → search **Yummy☆Surprise**.
6. Special Summon **Cooky☆Yummy**. Skip its ATK effect.

**Herald, then Borreload**
7. Synchro: Cupsy★Yummy Way + Cupsy → **Shamisen Samsara Sorrowcat** (Level 3 Tuner).
8. Synchro: Sorrowcat + Cooky → **Herald of the Arc Light** (Level 4).
9. **Sorrowcat (GY):** return Cupsy★Yummy Way to the Extra Deck → Special Summon Sorrowcat.
10. **Yummy☆Surprise:** Special Summon Marshmao from the GY → Marshmao adds Yummy☆Surprise back.
11. Synchro: Sorrowcat (3) + Herald (4) + Marshmao (1) → **Borreload Savage Dragon**: equip **Yummy★Snatchy** from the GY (1 counter).
12. **Herald (GY):** search **Illusion of Chaos**.

**Dark Magician part**
13. **Illusion of Chaos:** reveal it → add **Dark Magician, the Pharaoh's Servant**, put Illusion on top of your Deck.
14. **Servant:** reveal Yummy☆Surprise → Special Summon it → **Set The Gaze of Timaeus** from the Deck.
15. **Dark Magician of Destruction:** banish Servant → summon it → search **Dark Magical Curtain**.
16. **Curtain:** Special Summon **Witch of the Black Forest** from the Deck.
17. Link Witch → **Artemis**. **Witch (GY):** search **Lollipo☆Yummy**.
18. Special Summon **Lollipo☆Yummy** (Witch stops its effects this turn; it can still summon itself).

**Finish**
19. Synchro: Artemis (as a Level 1 Tuner) + Lollipo → **Lollipo★Yummy Way**: Special Summon 2 Yummies from the GY (effects negated).
20. Synchro: Lollipo★Yummy Way (2) + Dark Magician of Destruction (8) → **Baronne de Fleur**.
21. Link the 2 revived Yummies → **S:P Little Knight**. Skip its banish.
22. Set **Yummy☆Surprise**.

**End:** Baronne · Borreload (1 negate) · Little Knight · Mignon · set Yummy☆Surprise · set Gaze (her turn: target Dark Magician of Destruction in your GY → **Dragoon**).

- Curtain at step 16 lets her Special Summon a DARK Spellcaster from her hand or Deck too. Against Natasha, stop after step 15 (searching something else) if you'd rather not give her a free Dark Magician.

## Game log and lessons

### 19 Nov 2026 vs Rhino (HEROs): lost

- Going 2nd. Her turn-1 board: Contrast HERO Chaos, Masked HERO Dark Law, a Level 8 HERO, 1 set card.
- Turn 3: Imperm on Dark Law, TTT stole Contrast HERO Chaos, then Curtain → Servant, Ash + Servant → Baronne, Gaze → Dragoon. The planned 9,000 lethal left her on **1400**.
- Turn 5: down to Little Knight + Mind on Air with only Droll in hand, against 6 HERO monsters. Lost on her next turn.

**Lessons**
- **Dark Law** banishes every card going to your GY, which shuts off Mignon, Sorrowcat, Surprise and Gaze. Imperm only stops it for one turn, so that turn has to be lethal.
- **Dark Magical Curtain lets her Special Summon a DARK Spellcaster too.** In a lethal line it can hand her a blocker. Count her possible summon before relying on Curtain for damage. (Claude's turn-3 line missed this.)
- Going 2nd, **count damage against her LP before ending the turn**, and keep a Yummy Way Synchro in the GY so Spright Elf can revive it later.

### 20 Nov 2026 vs Utopia / Zexal (going 2nd): lost

- Her turn-1 board: Number F0: Utopic Future Zexal, Number F0: Utopic Draco Future, Number 99: Utopia Dragonar, Ultimate Leo Utopia Ray, Utopia Double, Onomato field spell, then Titanic Galaxy and Unbreakable Xyz Barrier.
- Draco Future negated Marshmao's summon from hand. The rest of the turn was negated too (Titanic Galaxy, Unbreakable Xyz Barrier).

**Lessons**
- **Yummy summons from hand are activated effects**, so Draco Future and Titanic-style negates can hit them. Bait with an effect you don't need first (Lollipo's GY shuffle).
- **Zexal stops targeting of her other cards** and steals a monster when you activate anything on the field. This deck has no non-targeting answer: concede early if there's no out.
- With no hand traps in the opening hand, this matchup is unwinnable going 2nd. Keep hand traps when deciding what to discard.

### 20 Nov 2026 vs Altergeist (going 2nd): won (opponent surrendered, turn 2)

- Hand: Lollipo, Pharaoh's Servant, Illusion of Chaos, Dark Magical Curtain ×2.
- Silquitous bounced Snatchy to the Extra Deck in response to its effect. Snatchy still placed Mignon. Curtain → Witch → **Artemis** made a new Link-1, so Mignon could revive Lollipo and the combo carried on. Witch can't search Cupsy (DEF too high), so it took Cooky.
- She surrendered once Servant hit the field.

**Lessons**
- If your Link-1 gets bounced or removed, Curtain → Witch → Artemis rebuilds it, and Mignon works again.
- **Magicians' Souls** (send Mind on Air from Deck) is a spare Level 1 for a second Yummy Way with Snatchy.
- Against a Trap deck, bait with Lollipo's effect first.

### 20 Nov 2026 vs unknown (going 2nd): won (turn 4)

- Hand: Cooky, Fuwalos, Acroquey, Imperm, Dominus Spark ×2. Her turn 1: one Level 2 monster.
- Acroquey destroyed her monster; built Sorrowcat → Herald → Little Knight → Servant → Dark Magician of Destruction → Curtain → Witch. Her Curtain summon gave her a Dark Magician.
- **Mistake (Claude's line):** S:P Little Knight took the free Extra Monster Zone, and its arrows point sideways, so **Artemis had no zone** and the Lollipo★Yummy Way → Baronne finish was lost. Linked Witch + Little Knight → Spright Elf instead, attacked her to 4700, then Gaze → Dragoon on her turn and Surprise → Marshmao in her End Phase. Won next turn.

**Lessons**
- **Check zones before every Link:** make the Link-1 (Artemis, Snatchy) **before** filling the Extra Monster Zone with a Link-2, or put the Link-2 where a free zone stays pointed to.
- Yummy☆Surprise's modes reset each turn: on her turn, summon Marshmao from the GY, and Marshmao gets Surprise back.

### 20 Nov 2026 vs Witchcrafter (going 1st, Drolled): won (opponent surrendered, turn 2)

- Hand: Lollipo, Maxx "C", TTT, Curtain, Imperm. She Drolled right after Cupsy★Yummy Way's search (Cupsy + Marshmao added, TTT discarded).
- **Drolled line (Line 7 idea, worked):** Cupsy + Marshmao from hand → Curtain → Mind on Air → Dark Magician of Destruction (search fails) → Cupsy★Yummy Way + Dark Magician of Destruction = **Baronne** → Cupsy → Snatchy → Snatchy + Marshmao = Lollipo★Yummy Way (revive Lollipo + Cupsy) → Lollipo★Yummy Way + Lollipo = Sorrowcat → Sorrowcat + Lollipo = **Herald** → Sorrowcat GY (return Lollipo★Yummy Way) revives itself. Set Imperm.
- End: Baronne, Herald, Sorrowcat, Mignon, set Imperm. Her turn: Super Poly fused her Marka + my Sorrowcat into Mudragon; **Herald negated Witchcrafter Potterie** and searched Illusion of Chaos. She surrendered.

**Lessons**
- **Under Droll, summon from the Deck instead of searching:** Curtain → Mind on Air → Dark Magician of Destruction still reaches Baronne.
- **Make Herald on your own turn** when Sorrowcat can then revive itself from the GY: one more body than waiting for Sorrowcat's Quick Synchro.
- **Super Poly** can use your monsters as material and can't be responded to: expect to lose a body to it.
