# Yummy Dragoon

**Status of the lines below:** Line 2 follows a published Japanese guide (appmedia, "Curtain Yummy", updated 18 Nov 2026), with the steps changed where Lewis's Extra Deck differs. Lines 1 and 3 are **built from card text and UNTESTED**. Card text checked 19 Nov 2026.

## How the deck works

- **Yummy engine.** Normal Summon any Yummy → Link it into **Yummy★Snatchy** (Link-1). Snatchy places the field spell **Mignon** from the Deck. Mignon revives a Level 1 Yummy. Snatchy (counts as a Level 1 Tuner) + that Yummy → a Level 2 **Yummy Way** Synchro.
- **Cupsy★Yummy Way** is the key Synchro: it searches **2 Yummy monsters**, then you discard 1.
- Small Yummy monsters **summon themselves from hand** while you control a Link-1 or a Level 2 Synchro (Marshmao: while all your monsters are LIGHT Beasts). Their summons don't start a chain.
- **Dark Magician package.** **Dark Magical Curtain** summons Dark Magician from the Deck and searches **The Gaze of Timaeus**. Gaze turns a Dark Magician on your field or in your GY into **Red-Eyes Dark Dragoon**.
- **Piri Reis Map** finds **Cupsy☆Yummy** (0 ATK). Its effects stay locked until you **Normal Summon** it, so Piri → Normal Summon Cupsy is a Yummy starter. It costs half your LP.

## Line 1: Yummy + Curtain → Baronne, Dragoon, Little Knight

UNTESTED, built from card text. Needs: any Yummy (Cooky in the example), Dark Magical Curtain, 1 spare card to discard.

1. Normal Summon **Cooky☆Yummy**. Skip its ATK effect (nothing to target going first).
2. Link Cooky → **Yummy★Snatchy**. Snatchy: place **Yummyusment☆Mignon** from the Deck.
3. **Mignon:** Special Summon Cooky from the GY.
4. Synchro: Snatchy (as a Level 1 Tuner) + Cooky → **Cupsy★Yummy Way**: add **Cupsy☆Yummy** + **Marshmao☆Yummy**, discard the spare.
5. Special Summon **Cupsy☆Yummy** from hand → search **Yummy☆Surprise**.
6. Special Summon **Marshmao☆Yummy** from hand. Skip its effect (no Yummy Spell/Trap in the GY yet).
7. **Dark Magical Curtain:** Special Summon **Dark Magician** from the Deck → add **The Gaze of Timaeus**.
8. **Dark Magician of Destruction:** banish Dark Magician (a Spell was activated this turn) → summon it from the Extra Deck. On summon: add a card that mentions Dark Magician (a 2nd Curtain for next turn).
9. Synchro: Cupsy★Yummy Way (Level 2 Tuner) + Dark Magician of Destruction (Level 8) → **Baronne de Fleur**.
10. **Gaze of Timaeus:** target Dark Magician of Destruction in the GY (it counts as Dark Magician there) → **Red-Eyes Dark Dragoon**.
11. Link Cupsy + Marshmao → **S:P Little Knight**. Skip its banish.
12. Set **Yummy☆Surprise**.

**End:** Baronne · Red-Eyes Dark Dragoon · Little Knight · Mignon · set Yummy☆Surprise.

- **Dragoon from Gaze is banished in the End Phase of the next turn**, so going first it guards her turn and then leaves.
- **Curtain lets her Special Summon a DARK Spellcaster from her hand or Deck too.** Against Natasha that's a free Dark Magician for her. Against her, think about Line 2 instead.
- **Check in game:** whether Gaze uses only the targeted Dark Magician as material (it shuffles it into the Deck), and that Dark Magician of Destruction in the GY can be its target.

## Line 2: Any Yummy alone → Herald of the Arc Light

Follows appmedia's route steps 1–12. Their next step uses Zaren, which isn't in this Extra Deck, so the line stops earlier.

1. Normal Summon **Cooky☆Yummy**.
2. Link Cooky → **Yummy★Snatchy**: place **Mignon** from the Deck.
3. **Mignon:** Special Summon Cooky from the GY.
4. Synchro: Snatchy + Cooky → **Cupsy★Yummy Way**: add **Cupsy☆Yummy** + **Marshmao☆Yummy**, discard 1.
5. Special Summon **Cupsy☆Yummy** → search **Yummy☆Surprise**.
6. Special Summon **Marshmao☆Yummy**.
7. Synchro: Cupsy★Yummy Way + Marshmao → **Shamisen Samsara Sorrowcat** (Level 3 Tuner).
8. Synchro: Sorrowcat + Cupsy☆Yummy → **Herald of the Arc Light** (Level 4).
9. **Sorrowcat (GY):** return Cupsy★Yummy Way to the Extra Deck → Special Summon Sorrowcat.
10. **Yummy☆Surprise:** Special Summon Marshmao from the GY → Marshmao adds Yummy☆Surprise back.
11. Set **Yummy☆Surprise**.

**End:** Herald of the Arc Light (Tribute it to negate a Spell/Trap or monster effect) · Sorrowcat (Synchro on her turn) · Marshmao · Mignon · set Yummy☆Surprise.

- While Herald is on the field, monsters sent from your hand or Deck to the GY are banished instead. Do your discards before Herald lands.
- **Piri Reis Map start:** Piri → add **Cupsy☆Yummy** → Normal Summon it (lifts the lock) → search a Yummy → then from step 2 with Cupsy instead of Cooky.

## Line 3: Curtain alone → Dragoon

UNTESTED, built from card text.

1. **Dark Magical Curtain:** Special Summon **Dark Magician** from the Deck → add **The Gaze of Timaeus**.
2. **Gaze of Timaeus:** target Dark Magician → **Red-Eyes Dark Dragoon**.

**End:** Dragoon (can't be targeted or destroyed by card effects; discard to negate once per turn) until the End Phase of her turn.

## Her turn: hand traps (from appmedia)

- **Maxx "C":** as soon as she summons a monster.
- **Ash:** on a search.
- **Imperm / Veiler:** on the monster whose effect starts her combo.
- **Dominus Spark from hand:** you can't activate effects of EARTH, WATER, FIRE or WIND monsters for the rest of the duel. Yummy (LIGHT) and Dark Magician (DARK) are fine, but **your Ash (FIRE), Droll (WIND), Fuwalos (WIND) and Maxx "C" (EARTH) go dead**. Set it instead when you can.

## When opponents play Yummy (appmedia)

- Yummy Special Summons from hand don't start a chain, so Maxx "C" works best when a Yummy is summoned.
- Ash Cupsy☆Yummy's or Cupsy★Yummy Way's search.
- Imperm/Veiler Cupsy☆Yummy's search or Snatchy's Synchro effect (Snatchy's is once per chain: use the hand trap after it resolves).
