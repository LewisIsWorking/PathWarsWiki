# Cards: Invoked

Paraphrased; follow the source for exact wording. Verified Nov 2026 unless noted.

## Main deck

- **Aleister the Invoker** – On Normal Summon (or flip), search Invocation. From hand: discard it to give a Fusion you control +1000 attack.
- **Invocation** – Fusion Summon using monsters from your hand or field; for an Invoked fusion, you can also banish monsters from your field and/or **either** player's GY. GY effect: target one of your **banished** "Aleister the Invoker"; shuffle Invocation into the Deck and add that Aleister. That GY effect is once per turn; the card itself isn't. ([source](https://www.db.yugioh-card.com/yugiohdb/card_search.action?ope=2&cid=12852&request_locale=en))
- **Invocation "Sword"** – Quick-Play. Fusion Summon using monsters you control; for an Invoked fusion, banished monsters can be used too (returned to the GY). GY: shuffle it back to add an Aleister or Invocation from the GY. Can't be activated the turn it's set.
- **Invocation "Grail"** (SR) – Exact text: "If you have an "Aleister" monster in your field, GY, or banishment: Activate 1 of these effects; ● Fusion Summon 1 "Invoked" Fusion Monster from your Extra Deck, by banishing 1 monster in your hand or field and 1 monster in your Deck as material. ● Fusion Summon 1 "Invoked" Fusion Monster from your Extra Deck, by banishing 1 monster you control and 1 face-up monster your opponent controls as material. You can only activate 1 "Invocation - "Grail"" per turn." **Dead without an Aleister already out.**
- **Magical Meltdown** – Searches Aleister.
- **Aiwass, Divine Spirit of the Law** – Banish it from the field: search Aleister and gain an extra Normal Summon of a Spellcaster. From hand: search only.
- **Sacred Spirit Sword Aiwass** (SR) – Exact text: "Activate 1 of these effects, but you cannot declare attacks for the rest of this turn, except with Fusion Monsters; ● Special Summon 1 "Aiwass, Divine Spirit of the Law" from your Deck or GY. ● Take 1 "Aleister" monster from your Deck, and either send it to the GY or banish it. ● Look at 3 random face-down cards in your opponent's Extra Deck, and if you do, banish 1 of them. You can only activate 1 "Sacred Spirit Sword Aiwass" per turn." The second mode turns on Grail and Virakam on its own.
- **Aleister the Reminiscent** (UR) – Exact text (start): "If this card is in your hand: You can target 1 Spellcaster monster or 1 Fusion Monster on the field; Special Summon this card, and if you do, the targeted monster gains 1000 ATK until the end of this turn. If this card is Normal or Special Summoned, or flipped face-up: You can banish 1 "Invoked" monster from your Extra Deck; add 1 "Invocation", or 1 Spell that mentions it, from your Deck to your hand." It is an "Aleister" monster (Grail, Virakam, Baybarron, Sorath) but not "Aleister the Invoker" (not Mechaba, Oceanus, Caliga, Raidjin, Omega, or Invocation's GY effect).
- **Virakam, the Divine Spirit of Artifice** – Level 3, 1000/1000. Special Summon from hand if you have an Aleister monster on your field or in your GY. On Special Summon: Set Invocation or a Spell that mentions it from the Deck. Quick Effect: negates when they respond to your Fusion Monster's effect.
- **Crowley, the Gifted Magistus** – Counts as Aleister for fusion material only. Summons itself when added from Deck to hand.
- **Gamma Summon** – Normal Trap. Banish an Aleister or Fusion from the GY to summon an Invoked; it's banished in the End Phase.

## Links

- **Aleister the Invoker of Madness** – 2 monsters with different Types **and** different Attributes. When you Fusion Summon: discard a card to search Invocation.
- **Artemis, the Magistus Moon Maiden** – Link-1 Spellcaster; its search needs another Magistus summoned.
- **S:P Little Knight** – Link-2, 2 Effect Monsters. If Link Summoned with a Fusion/Synchro/Xyz/Link as material: banish a card on the field or in either GY, **but your monsters can't attack directly this turn**. Quick Effect when they activate something: banish 2 face-up monsters (one yours) until the End Phase. Each once per turn.
- **Secure Gardna** – Needs a Cyberse Link as material; blocks damage once per turn.

## Supporting Extra Deck

- **Magistus Chorozo** – Level 4 DARK Spellcaster Fusion Tuner; 1 Fusion/Synchro/Xyz/Link + 1 Spellcaster. Every Baronne de Fleur route in Taffy's spreadsheet goes through it. ([source](https://www.yugiohcardguide.com/single/magistus-chorozo.html))
