# Cards: staples and generic cards

Paraphrased; follow the source for exact wording. Verified Nov 2026.

- **Forbidden Droplet** – Quick-Play. Send any number of other cards from your hand/field to the GY; **choose** (not target) that many of their Effect Monsters: halved attack and negated until end of turn. They can't respond with cards of the same original type as the ones you sent. One per turn. Nothing leaves the field, so "leaves the field" triggers don't fire. ([source](https://ygoprodeck.com/card/forbidden-droplet-11026))
- **Ghost Belle & Haunted Mansion** – Discard to negate an activation that adds a GY card to the hand/Deck/Extra Deck, Special Summons from the GY, or banishes from the GY. Once per turn. ([source](https://ygoprodeck.com/card/ghost-belle-haunted-mansion-9263))
- **Infinite Impermanence** – Can be activated from hand only if you control **no cards**. Once one Imperm is on your field you control a card, so a second can't come from hand.
- **Ash Blossom & Joyous Spring** – Once per turn.
- **Effect Veiler** – Main Phase only.
- **Mulcharmy Fuwalos** – Only while you control no cards.
- **Dark Magical Curtain** – Quick-Play. Each player can Special Summon a DARK Spellcaster from hand or Deck (its effects can't be activated this turn). If yours was originally named "Dark Magician" or "Dark Magician Girl", search a Spell/Trap that mentions Dark Magician. One per turn. ([source](https://ygoprodeck.com/card/dark-magical-curtain-15566))
- **Dark Magician, the Pharaoh's Servant** – Its name becomes "Dark Magician" on the field and in the GY. From hand: reveal a Spell to Special Summon it, then you can Set a Dark Magician Spell/Trap from the Deck. Quick Effect: discard a Spell to destroy all their Spells/Traps. Only 1 of these effects per turn. ([source](https://www.yugiohcardguide.com/single/dark-magician-the-pharaohs-servant.html))
- **The Gaze of Timaeus** – Quick-Play. Target Dark Magician or Dark Magician Girl on your field or in your GY; Fusion Summon a Fusion Monster that lists it, shuffling it into the Deck as material. That monster is **banished in the End Phase of the next turn**. One per turn.
