# Cards: opponent decks

Paraphrased; follow the source for exact wording. Verified Nov 2026.

## Dark Magician / Horus (Natasha)

- **Red-Eyes Dark Dragoon** – Dark Magician + Red-Eyes Black Dragon or a Dragon Effect Monster. Can't be destroyed by card effects; neither player can target it. Main Phase: destroy one of your monsters and burn for its original attack (uses = Normal Monsters used as material). Once per turn Quick Effect: discard a card to negate an activation, destroy it, gain 1000 attack. ([source](https://ygoprodeck.com/card/red-eyes-dark-dragoon-10694))
- **King's Sarcophagus** – Continuous Spell. Her Horus monsters can't be destroyed by effects that **don't** target them. Send a card from hand + a Horus monster from Deck to the GY, up to four times per turn. Once per turn, when her Horus monster battles yours, yours is sent to the GY at the start of the Damage Step. ([source](https://ygoprodeck.com/card/king-s-sarcophagus-14003))
- **The four Horus monsters** – all revive themselves from her GY while she controls King's Sarcophagus, and all trigger when *another* of her cards leaves the field by your effect. ([source](https://ygorganization.com/something2dance2/))

| Monster | Stats | Trigger when her card leaves by your effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Imsety | DARK Lv8, 3000/1800 | Sends 1 card on the field to the GY. Also: discard it + 1 card to search Sarcophagus and draw 1 |
| Duamutef | WATER Lv8, 0/0, +1200 per Horus she controls | Draws 1 per differently named monster in her Main Monster Zones |
| Qebehsenuef | EARTH Lv8, 2500/2000 | This turn you can't attack or target her Horus monsters |
| Hapi | WIND Lv8, 2400/1600 | Returns 2 cards from GYs/banished to hand or Deck |

- **Dark Magician Girl the Magician's Apprentice** – Level 6 DARK Spellcaster, 2000/1700. Discard a card to Special Summon it from hand; its name becomes "Dark Magician Girl" on the field. On Normal/Special Summon: search Shining Sarcophagus. ([source](https://ygoprodeck.com/card/dark-magician-girl-the-magician-s-apprentice-14883))
- **Dark Magician of Destruction** – Level 8 DARK Spellcaster Fusion, 2800/2600. Dark Magician + a LIGHT or DARK monster; or Special Summon from the Extra Deck in a turn a Spell was activated by banishing a Level 6+ DARK Spellcaster you control. Its name becomes "Dark Magician" on the field and in the GY. On Special Summon: search Dark Magician or a card that mentions it (e.g. Gaze of Timaeus). ([source](https://www.yugiohcardguide.com/single/dark-magician-of-destruction.html))
- **Horus Dragoon route (Master I list, Apr 2026):** Apprentice → banish it for Dark Magician of Destruction (King's Sarcophagus counts as the Spell) → search Gaze → Gaze turns Destruction into Dragoon.
- **Horus the Black Flame Deity** – Rarely played. If her Horus monster or Sarcophagus leaves the field by your effect, Special Summon from hand and send all other monsters to the GY. ([source](https://facetofacegames.com/products/horus-the-black-flame-deity-phni-en018-secret-rare-1st-edition))

## Cyber Dragon (Jack)

- **Cyber Dragon Infinity** – Rank 6, 3 Level 6 LIGHT Machines (or Xyz Summon over Cyber Dragon Nova). +200 attack per material. Once per turn: attach a face-up **Attack Position** monster as material. Once per turn Quick Effect: detach to negate and destroy. ([source](https://ygoprodeck.com/card/cyber-dragon-infinity-904))
- **Chimeratech Fortress Dragon** – Cyber Dragon + 1+ Machines, summoned by sending them from **either** field to the GY. 1000 attack per material. A summon procedure, not an effect. ([source](https://www.yugiohcardguide.com/single/chimeratech-fortress-dragon.html))
- **Chimeratech Megafleet Dragon** – A Cyber Dragon monster + 1+ monsters in the **Extra Monster Zones**, sent from either field. 1200 attack per material. A summon procedure, not an effect. ([source](https://ygoprodeck.com/card/chimeratech-megafleet-dragon-8972))

## Labrynth

- **Lady Labrynth of the Silver Castle** – Can't be destroyed by your effects; can't be targeted while she controls a Set card. Quick Effect from hand after a Labrynth card or Normal Trap: Special Summon. When a Normal Trap activates: Set a different Normal Trap from the Deck. Each once per turn. ([source](https://ygoprodeck.com/card/lady-labrynth-of-the-silver-castle-13386))

## Seen on ladder

- **Radiant Typhoon Fonix** – Summons itself when a Quick-Play is activated; shuffles 2 cards back.
- **Vingolf's Blessing** – Sends a LIGHT Fairy on activation.
- **Gem-Knight Lady Rose Diamond** – Destroys your face-up card when you activate a monster effect on their turn.
- **Tenpai Dragon Chundra** – Quick Synchro in the Battle Phase; summons a FIRE Dragon from the Deck when battling.
- **Dracotail Horn** – Normal Trap: returns an Attack Position monster to hand/Extra Deck.
- **Ultimate Leo Utopia Ray** – Detach to equip a ZW monster from Deck/Extra Deck (an equip, not a search).
