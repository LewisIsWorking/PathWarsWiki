# Card database

Verified card notes for Master Duel, added as cards come up in games and deck prep.

How entries work:

- The Horus, Shining Sarcophagus and Dark Magician pages quote the **exact card text**. Older pages are paraphrased.
- Each paraphrased entry is a **paraphrase** of the card's text, checked against the source linked beside it. Open the source for the exact wording.
- "Verified" is the date the text was checked. Re-check after errata or new Master Duel versions.
- Entries only cover what matters for play: materials, effects, once-per-turn limits and catches.
