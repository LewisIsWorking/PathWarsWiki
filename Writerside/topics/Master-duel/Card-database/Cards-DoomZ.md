# Cards: DoomZ / Power Patron

Paraphrased; follow the source for exact wording. Verified Oct–Nov 2026.

- **DoomZ Raiders** – Main Phase: destroy another DoomZ card in your hand or face-up field, then add or Special Summon a DoomZ monster from the Deck. After that, you can only summon Xyz from the Extra Deck this turn. If destroyed by a card effect: destroy a face-up monster on the field. One Raiders effect per turn. ([source](https://www.db.yugioh-card.com/yugiohdb/card_search.action?ope=2&cid=21774))
- **DoomZ V Five – Amalthe** – If Normal/Special Summoned, or destroyed by a card effect: search a DoomZ monster other than Amalthe. Battle destruction doesn't trigger it.
- **Elara, Amalthe, Drastea (shared effect)** – Quick Effect while equipped with an Equip Card: Special Summon a WIND Machine Xyz with the same Rank as its Level and attach it plus its equips as material (treated as an Xyz Summon).
- **Drastea** – From hand: destroy a DoomZ card you control, summon itself and equip an Equip Spell from the Deck (D.O.O.M.D.U.R.G.). Can then become the Rank 8 WIND Machine Xyz Drastrius.
- **DoomZ Break – Diactorus** (Rank 4) – On Special Summon: destroy a DoomZ card in your hand or face-up field, then destroy a monster on the field. If destroyed with DoomZ or Medius as material: add or send an Equip Spell from the Deck. ([source](https://yugipedia.com/wiki/DoomZ))
- **DoomZ XIII Over – Graflareio** (Rank 5) – Can be Xyz Summoned over another DoomZ Xyz. Searches a DoomZ Spell/Trap, then destroys a card on the field.
- **DoomZ Destruction** – Trap. Targets a DoomZ Xyz only; Jupiter doesn't count, and Jupiter can't re-equip it from the GY.
- **Medius the Pure** – On Normal/Special Summon: add or Special Summon a Power Patron monster from the Deck. GY: shuffle a monster from your hand or face-up field into the Deck to Special Summon it; banished when it leaves.
- **Jupredo the Shademachine Power Patron** – Always treated as a DoomZ card. Banish the top 3 cards of your Deck face-down: destroy it and Special Summon Jupiter the Power Patron of Destruction from the Extra Deck (treated as an Xyz Summon), then you can attach a card from your hand. If added to the Extra Deck face-up: search a Power Patron or DoomZ Spell/Trap. Each once per turn. ([source](https://www.db.yugioh-card.com/yugiohdb/member_deck.action?cgid=bc823565c06b1b6bc466e6efc7967d8e&dno=39&request_locale=en))
- **Vidolium the Unstable Power Patron of Unity** – Pendulum Scale 11. Destroy it and Special Summon a Power Patron from hand or GY; or destroy it and Level 12 Power Patrons you control gain 5000 attack this turn. Once per turn. Main-deck summon needs a Fusion, a Synchro and an Xyz returned to the Extra Deck. Added face-up to the Extra Deck: search a Power Patron.

Matchup notes: almost all DoomZ removal is **destruction**, and every DoomZ Xyz is a WIND **Machine**, so Chimeratech Fortress Dragon can use them as material.
