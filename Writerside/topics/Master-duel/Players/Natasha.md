## Rank 8 Xyz with 2 Horus (9 Nov 2026)

- Turning 2 Horus into an Xyz gives up King's Sarcophagus protection (it only covers Horus monsters), Sarcophagus's battle effect and both Horus triggers. Each Horus can only be revived from the GY once per turn per name, so only Xyz with Horus she can't otherwise use.
- Proven Horus lists use Number 90: Galaxy-Eyes Photon Lord and Number 38 (Fearnagae, WCS 2026 qualifiers, June 2026); a Horus guide also lists Dingirsu, Number 68 and Coach King Giantrainer. She owns none of these. Number 90 is the craft to aim for.
- From what she owns: **Hieratic Sky Dragon Overlord** on turn 1 (negates a targeting effect on her field, GY or banishment, covering the gap in Sarcophagus's protection). **Hieratic Sun Dragon Overlord** when going second (tributes to destroy). **Aegaion** against Invoked: Lewis banishes his own Fusions, which Aegaion can return to destroy one of his Fusions on the field, on either turn.

## Super Poly into her board (card text checked 20 Nov 2026)

- **Her Attributes:** Dragoon DARK, Imsety DARK, Duamutef WATER, Qebehsenuef EARTH, Hapi WIND, Horus the Black Flame Deity FIRE. So no single Attribute covers her board, except Elysium (it's every Attribute).
- **Best target: Dragoon.** Super Poly can't be responded to, so Dragoon can't negate it. Dragoon isn't a "Horus" monster, so it doesn't trigger Black Flame Deity.
- **Watch Horus the Black Flame Deity in her hand:** when a face-up Horus monster or King's Sarcophagus leaves her field by your effect, it Special Summons itself and can send **all other monsters on the field** to the GY (yours too). Once per turn. So trigger it early, while your field holds nothing you need. Fusing or banishing a Son, or removing King's Sarcophagus, triggers it.
- **Line (Invoked):** make an Invoked Fusion (e.g. Mechaba) → **Super Poly** it + Dragoon → **Invoked Elysium** → Elysium targets **itself** → banishes itself and all her monsters → Black Flame Deity lands and wipes the rest of the field → rebuild with what's left. Aleister the Invoker isn't an "Invoked" monster, so it can't be Elysium's material.
- **No Invoked Fusion yet:** Super Poly **Aleister + Dragoon → Invoked Oceanus** in the Extra Monster Zone. While it's there, her monsters sent to the GY are banished instead, so the Sons can't revive.
- **Aeon** (your Invoked Fusion + Dragoon, 2 materials) banishes 2 cards: both Sarcophagi switch off the Sons' revives and protection, but removing King's Sarcophagus triggers Black Flame Deity.

## Lewis's other decks vs her (card text checked 20 Nov 2026, untested)

- **Exosister: best on paper.** Her Sons revive from the GY, which "moves a card out of the GY": every Exosister main-deck monster can then Xyz Summon on her turn. **Kaspitell** (Xyz Summoned with an Exosister): neither player can Special Summon from the GY for the rest of the turn, so the Sons stop. **Asophiel**: neither player can activate effects in the GY that turn. **Magnifica**: banish 1 card she controls without targeting (gets Dragoon). Exosister Xyz can't be destroyed by effects of monsters Special Summoned from the GY.
- **Mitsurugi:** Wousu Tributes one of her monsters as a summoning cost (no targeting, no destruction: gets Dragoon). Murakumo's destroy-all mostly misses (Dragoon immune, King's Sarcophagus protects the Sons from non-targeting destruction).
- **Avoid decks that Special Summon from the GY a lot** (Tearlaments, Orcust, Snake-Eye): **Shining Sarcophagus** sends a monster you Special Summon from the GY back (she discards a Spell). Invoked (Sorath) and Yummy (Mignon, Sorrowcat, Surprise) do this too, so expect it once per turn.

## Her Dark Magical Curtain gives you a summon too (card text checked 20 Nov 2026)

Curtain: each player can Special Summon 1 DARK Spellcaster from hand or Deck, **but neither player can activate their effects this turn**. So pick a body you can use with a Quick-Play or as material, not one that needs its own effect.

- **Yummy:** take **Dark Magician, the Pharaoh's Servant** from the Deck. It counts as "Dark Magician", so a set **Gaze of Timaeus** turns it into **Dragoon on her turn** (Dragoon itself can use its effects). Without Gaze, it's still a 2500 body and next turn's Dark Magician of Destruction material. Mind on Air is the alternative (her hand stays revealed while it's on the field).
- **Invoked:** take **Aleister the Invoker** (or Reminiscent). With a set **Super Poly**: Aleister + her Dark Magician or Dragoon (both DARK) → **Invoked Oceanus** on her turn, and she can't respond. With a set **Invoke Sword**: Aleister + a banished LIGHT → **Mechaba** on her turn.
- No new cards needed: both decks already run these.

## Her hand traps (from her 9 Nov list)

Ash ×2, Droll ×2, Maxx "C" ×1, Ghost Mourner ×1, Forbidden Droplet ×1, Triple Tactics Talent ×1, Triple Tactics Thrust ×1.

- **Crossout Designator: name Droll against her.** Droll stops every search for the rest of the turn; Ash stops one effect.
- **Lead with the search you can afford to lose.** If Droll lands, switch to the "Drolled" line (Invoked cheat sheet, Yummy Line 7). Droll doesn't stop Setting from the Deck, summoning from the Deck, or adding from the GY or banishment.
- **Triple Tactics Talent** goes live as soon as she uses a monster hand trap on your turn.
- **Maxx "C":** decide before your combo whether to keep going (she draws per Special Summon) or stop at a smaller board.
- **Her Droplet** negates without targeting, so a Dragoon or Baronne can still be switched off on her turn.
- **Puzzles:** her hand has Ash and Droll, but EDOPro's AI may not use them sensibly. When testing, play as if she Drolls your first search and Ashes your best effect.

## Hand simulation: Witchcrafter vs Rikka into her board (21 Nov 2026, rough model)

300,000 random going-second hands (6 cards) per deck. Witchcrafter = Lewis's WitchCrafterRd1604 (40); Rikka = xemnas43's Rating Duels list (1605). Same rule as the puzzles: each of your hand traps removes one piece of her board on her turn, Dragoon first; on your turn she still holds Ash + Droll. This is a hand-scoring model from card text, not a game engine.

| | Witchcrafter | Rikka |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Full break (Dragoon handled + her board wiped, combo survives her Ash/Droll) | 18% | 54% |
| Partial (Dragoon handled, combo runs) | 33% | n/a |
| Brick | 6% | 10% |

- **Her weak point is Black Flame Deity:** when a Son leaves her field by your effect, it wipes **all other monsters**, including her own Sons and Dragoon. Any non-targeting removal of a Son (Super Polymerization, Rikka Konkon's tribute) clears her side.
- **Witchcraft outs:** Super Polymerization (Dragoon + a Son, no responses) and Unveiling + Madame Verre (negates all her monsters). Opening hand traps also pull Dragoon off her board.
- **Rikka is better on paper** (Konkon tributes as a cost, so Dragoon can't stop it, and Rikka runs 13 hand traps), but Lewis doesn't own it (5 URs short).
