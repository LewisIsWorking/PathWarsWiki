# C06-Tarsus-Longstaff

**Player:** Buffet Raider (@buffetraider)
**Campaign:** #6 Kibwe

---

## Overview

Tarsus Longstaff is a staff-fighting spellcaster who has travelled with the Kibwe party since Holy Xatramba.

The party stat block lists him as **Gnoll / Dromaar**, dual-classed **Wizard - Magus**.

He fights with his staff, which twists, grows and swells with magic, and channels spells through it with Spellstrike and Arcane Cascade.

He keeps notes, chalk and a spellbook in his backpack and is usually the first to study runes, wards and magical writing.

---

## Backstory

In Buffet Raider's own words (Telegram, 2026-11-03):
"Tarsus is an apprentice bonespeaker that was traveling the region to learn more about the world and magic, the rumors of a curse affecting the nearby human settlement piqued his interest."

---

## Ancestry: What the Story Shows

The transcripts never say outright that Tarsus is a kholo (gnoll). It is only implied, and fits his training as a bonespeaker:

- The GM tells him: "Tarsus sweat drips from your fur."
- When he studies Lorn's changed form, "His hackles raise and he occasionally growls to himself".
- He says of Lorn: "his bones belong to the pack once he's done with them."
- Later: "As much as I hate to see the parts that ARE gnoll bones defaced like that, it's not like there's anything I can do about it."
- Fierce Leopard "turns away from his fellow Kholo" after Tarsus speaks.

---

## Travelling Companions

Tarsus arrived at Holy Xatramba with "his two traveling companions", Fierce Leopard and Tiny.

At the entrance he stopped the dwarf Padraig Dream-Soup: "Hey, dwarf! Hold up a sec. You with the first wave of adventurers they sent from Kibwe?"

## Key Events in Part 1 (Holy Xatramba)

### Reading the Lab Notes

Tarsus read two languages on the walls.
One was Sin-speak, "intermingled with magic". The other "screws with my head if I try to read it."

He read the Sin-speak as lab notes about forcing shapechanging spells on large groups of people, called "bio-hacking", "working with ooze form in particular".

"Damn, magical languages are worse than trying to copy spells from a wizard with awful penmanship."

### The Contract

Before anyone signed the contract offered in Holy Xatramba, Tarsus asked to read it first:
"I just want to verify the scope of the commitment we're making here."

Lorn signed it. When Tarsus saw Lorn's changed, crystalline form, he asked Ademe: "Please tell me this is reversible."

### The Forge and the Ritual Room

Tarsus and Daichi completed a suit of moonlit chain armour in the plaza's forging room.

He cast Pillar of Water to break up a cultist ritual: "Small price to pay to interrupt whatever ritual they were preparing."

On the halfling question, he offered: "Um, actually, humans are dire halflings. I thought that was common knowledge."

### Reviving Curse Breaker

Tarsus studied the dormant Curse Breaker statue and gave the party its diagnosis:
"Curse Breaker appears to have spent all of its magic, and even burned part of its soul to break that curse earlier."

He proposed a ritual of "four spellcasters", and suggested using Theron instead of Eda: "Punishment fitting the crime and whatnot."

He then took part in the ritual and followed Eda through her portal to Kibwe.

### The Fox and Aberith

A black fox delivered a letter signed by Aberith.
Tarsus followed the fox, traced the heptagram, laid out the coins and spoke the chant.

A voice answered in his head. Tarsus explained that the party had no options "that don't involve a member of our party dying."

The voice said it could not give the answer away for free, and that it did not "speak in person with my clients until they are willing to discuss the terms of the contract".

No terms were ever agreed.

## Key Events in Part 2 (Outside the Walls)

When the party weighed Tal'lysae's offer of alliance, Tarsus argued for dealing with the curse first:
"May as well deal with what's in front of us so that we're ready for whatever comes next, especially knowing we have time to prepare."

He also named a thread he means to come back to:
"tracking down the green flames or whatever that secret society calls themselves will be necessary at some point."

## Key Events in Part 3 (The Sun Temple)

### The White Flame Slime

Tarsus spellstruck the giant slime with lightning.
When it tried to escape through a dimensional rift, he recognised the spell as dimension door and countered it, collapsing both rifts.

### The Watchtower

Tarsus volunteered to stay alone and help the Nexian mages with their ritual while the others went on.
In the end the whole party joined in, and Tarsus held the roof against the slimes.

A slime broke several of his ribs. When the ritual ended he called out: "Can you give us a minute before we start anything else? I can't be the only one that needs a medic after that."

Earlier in the fight, Ji Yun had healed him with Soothe.

### Carrying Tal'lysae

When the tower began to grow and rebuild itself, Tarsus carried Tal'lysae out to the street.
He sat her against a wall and sat down beside her.

### Moffat's Offer

Envoy Moffat, before leaving with the Nexians for a backup shelter, told him:
"Tarsus, if you ever wish to join Nex, when this is all over, there's a position for you as a high mage."

No answer from Tarsus appears in the transcripts so far.

### Into the Temple

- Recognised the guards on the approach as Red Caps.
- Cast Loose Time's Arrow to quicken the party.
- Repaired Daichi's armour with scrap metal after the Golden Guardian fight.
- Chased the fat gnome and called down a Thunderstrike on him.
- Became master of the dual-mode Rod of Wonder.
- In the Chamber of Scribing, studied the slabs and their lightning runes.
