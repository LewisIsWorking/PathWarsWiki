# C06-Ji-Yun

**Player:** Caelum (Thiên) (@Thien_Ming)
**Campaign:** #6 Kibwe

---

## Overview

Ji Yun is a summoner from Tian Xia who joined the party on the roof of the watchtower during the Nexian mages' ritual.

The party stat block lists him as **Yaoguai / Born of Vegetation (Fungus)**, dual-classed **Eldamon Trainer - Summoner**.

---

## Who He Is

He introduced himself once the ritual was over. He formed the Zi Wu hand seal and named himself:
"emissary of the Ji family from Tianjing."

He explained his name: Ji "as in discipline", Yun "as in sunlight".

He said why he came:
"I came to request Kibwe's aid regarding an ancient seal, but seeing Kibwe's current state... allow me to offer my help instead."

He has not yet said what the seal is. Tal'lysae later wondered aloud to Tarsus: "I wonder what seal the Tian Xia, Ji Yun, was talking about..."

---

## Companions

### The Blood God Eidolon

Ji Yun's eidolon, which he acts together with in battle. It shares his hit points and stamina.

### Jiufeng

A fire eldamon. "Ji Yun pull out a drawing of a nine-headed bird and suddenly a nine-headed bird bursts forth from the paper."
Jiufeng's flamethrower has burned through slimes and guard squads.

### Feng

An eldamon that heals. In Ji Yun's words, after it healed Kieran, it was "The monstrosity named Feng". Kieran could sense "something warm and kind in its eyes".
When it was done, it turned into light particles and flowed back into the paper scroll in Ji Yun's hand, forming an image of itself.

The transcripts do not make clear whether Feng and Jiufeng are the same creature.

### The Daemon Familiar

Ji Yun compares his daemon familiar to Gu.
Before leaving the watchtower, he had his slime eat one of the slimes and his familiar absorb the soul of the big slime.

## Key Events in Part 3 (The Sun Temple)

### Arrival at the Watchtower

"Ji Yun sensed a magical wave from the watchtower and flee up to it. "Looks like you guys need some help" he said, a faint smile playing at his lips."

### The Party's Healer

- Cast Soothe on Tarsus.
- Gave Daichi a Life Buffer while he was dying.
- Used Healing Motes and Vital Touch, healing Kieran for 48.
- Aided the party with "You can do it!".

### The Fulu Talismans

Before the Sun Temple, Ji Yun asked the party for the slime corpses:
"I intend to refine their essence into ink for crafting fulu talismans."

He explained that the talismans are weak alone, but "if we cast twenty at once, their power shall converge. And more importantly, these fulu stick easily to the cursed slime bodies."

Tal'lysae's next replies included: "Interesting. I think your skills will have long term applications in this city..."
The transcript does not show which post that line answered, but it came between the fulu request and Tal'lysae handing over the slime she had gathered.
The haul was loaded onto the party mule.

### The Temple

- Burned and killed the remains of the fourth guard legion with Jiufeng.
- Was badly hurt by acid in the fight with the gnomes, at one point down to 3 of 50 hit points. Tal'lysae healed him during that fight.
- In the Chamber of Scribing, a stone scribing table swapped places with his eidolon.
