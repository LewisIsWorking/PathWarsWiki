# C06-Master-Cho-Kobo

**Player:** Bruce (@Ravnos1)
**Campaign:** #6 Kibwe

---

## Overview

Master Cho Kobo is a black-feathered tengu who joined the party in the streets of Kibwe, during the fight with the White Flame Merchant Slime.

The party stat block lists him as **Tengu / Jinxed Tengu**, dual-classed **Thaumaturge - Monk**.

He is a master of Esoteric Lore. Once per day, as a jinxed tengu, he can use a reaction to counter a fortune or misfortune effect.

---

## Appearance and Gear

His first appearance: "a lone figure dressed in travel robes and wearing a large hat to shade from the sun stood a black feathered Tengu."

Bottles, bones and other items dangle from his over robe and clink together when he walks.

- **Gale Blade:** his sword.
- **Gakugang bow:** his bow, for range.
- **Salt:** he dusts his blade with it against the slime: "Slime like being, it is best to not let you spread your wickedness and thus I bind you with salt."
- **Feather fan:** used "to waft good fortune around him".
- **Silver coated bones.**
- **A mirror on a cord:** "Master CHo knows that mirrors can trap evil spirits and the energy of curses, just as much as they can show the true self."

---

## Who He Is

He introduced himself after the slime fight:
"Hello, I was passing through, when I saw the potential danger your team was in. If you need any additional assistance please let me know and I may be able to fill that need."

Later he told the party his trade:
"I am a bounty hunter by trade. Curse hunting has been very profitable since this all began."

Tal'lysae had already asked him: "Is that what YOU are? Curse Hunter?"

---

## Key Events in Part 3 (The Sun Temple)

### The White Flame Slime

Cho exploited the slime's vulnerability, choosing Mortal Weakness, and struck it from behind with a flurry of blows.

### The Watchtower Ritual

At first he suggested the temple might hold answers: "However as the gods seem to be involved as well, perhaps heading to the temple may reveal new insights that we do not have. Yes?"

When the party joined the ritual, he took a place in it with his Esoteric Lore.
He held his feather fan and silver bones and chanted in Tengu, "calls upon the various gods of magic seeking their aid".

In a later round he spun his mirror on its cord until a mirror duplicate of himself appeared, and used it to deflect the curse's negative energy.

His concentration slipped once, and he forced it back.

### The Flower

When Kieran picked the slime flower from the watchtower, Cho sensed that it was "a very, very high-level occult item".
He felt a shift, as if Kieran had opened a door through which occult and primal magic became stronger.

He warned Kieran: "Be gentle, sir. There is a lot of otherworldly power within."

### Watching Kieran

When Kieran left the tower, Cho followed him:
"I sense you are somehow connected to the city somehow. I willl continue to observe you to monitor as I can. To make sure that this is not a new curse waiting to unfold."

### The Temple Grounds

Outside the temple he answered a stranger's question with his fan:
"There is more to liberate than just books here. it is the soul of this city and perhaps this world that we fight for."

He kept watch while the others recovered from the Golden Guardian fight: "Good job friends. Take what time you need."

He heard Halfling and Dwarvish being spoken through the temple's thick doors.

### The Guard Legion

Against the small guard legion, his Sympathetic Vulnerabilities applied his exploit to every troop in the fight.
He called out: "If you have grenades of other splash weapons use it against them, As they move in formation they cannot dodge as easily."
