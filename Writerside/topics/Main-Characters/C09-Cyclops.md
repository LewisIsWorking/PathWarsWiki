### Summary
  Cyclops is a dwarven space pirate currently employed by the crew that has taken the Stargazers captive. Cyclops is the nickname she got because of the visor she wears, which gives the illusion of having a singular red eye. She is an Inventor and works as an engineer (though the crew also treats her as a general maintenance worker and handyman).
### Relations
  Captain Vex Ashburn
    Technically her boss. She was somewhat coerced into joining the crew when her own ship was attacked. Vex gave Cyclops to join her crew after noticing the strange technology in Cyclops’ possession.
  Mox Callan “The Whisper”
    Second in command of the pirate crew. Cyclops steers clear of them whenever possible, and when she’s forced to interact with them she tries to end the conversation quickly. As Oofy has shared with the Stargazers, Mox usually remains on the “Iron Tongue” with most of the crew.
  Oofy
    The cleaning robot of the pirate crew, and Cyclops’s only friend. They met when Oofy was in need of repairs and Cyclops was tasked with fixing him. Oofy is aware that Cyclops’s real name is Andromeda.
