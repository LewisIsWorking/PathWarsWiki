# C06-Daichi-Kenshin

**Player:** Anthony (@MrNegetZ)
**Campaign:** #6 Kibwe

---

## Overview

Daichi Kenshin is a staff-fighting spellcaster and trap-breaker who has been with the Kibwe party since Holy Xatramba.

The party stat block lists him as **Kitsune / Dark Fields**, dual-classed **Rogue - Magus**.

He fights with a staff, using Spellstrike, Arcane Cascade and trips, carries infiltrator tools for traps and wiring, and can heal with lay on hands.

He has master Kibwe Lore.

He also often takes Eda's turns at the table.

---

## Working for Kibwe

Daichi has framed the mission as city business from the start.

To the prisoners in Holy Xatramba:
"Ok everyone! First off, good new! You're going to be free from your unjust imprisonment as we are here under the orders of the Kibwe city council and are currently raiding the place."

At the watchtower, Envoy Moffat of Nex recognised Daichi, and seemingly only Daichi, from earlier in the campaign.

---

## Key Events in Part 1 (Holy Xatramba)

### Asking the Hard Questions

Daichi challenged the human who said she had been "monitoring the experiment":
"It just crossed my mind but you are human aren't you? How come you're not a slime yet?"

When the party was offered two bad outcomes, he pushed back:
"How about an outcome where we don't die a slime and don't become blood monsters either? Ever thought of that?"

### The Forge

Daichi and Tarsus completed a suit of moonlit chain armour in the plaza's forging room.

### Theron

Daichi critically tripped Theron, demoralised him ("Was that all? Well... Face smashing time") and spellstruck him with a blazing bolt.
Afterwards he pulled out a rope: "We'll need to tie this on you".

### Curse Breaker

Daichi did not like the idea of losing Eda, but agreed to the ritual:
"I'll help with the ritual if Eda is ready to go through with it."

He also asked whether Ademe could get a message to the city, to "ask the city officials to organise a coordinated prayer effort for curse breaker."

When Kieran's slime plan for Eda went ahead, he warned:
"I hope this works and you didn't just fuck up everything by trusting slime..."

---

## Key Events in Part 3 (The Sun Temple)

### The White Flame Slime

The slime engulfed Daichi and dragged him through its portals twice, leaving him badly hurt.

### The Watchtower

Daichi used his Kibwe Lore in the ritual.
He drew a map of the city and used it to guide the orb's discharge through Kibwe "in the less destructive way possible", then redrew the path as the ritual went on.

A slime stunned him and he went down, dying. Ji Yun's eldamon gave him a Life Buffer, he recovered on a hero point, healed himself and went back to fighting:
"Alright let's clean the place up."

### The Golden Guardian

A cat-like Golden Guardian leapt on Daichi when he opened the door and broke his breastplate.
"Ugh, this damn cat broke my armor! Anyone good at repairing stuff here?"

Tarsus repaired it.
"Note to self: Let Fierce Open the doors next time..."

### Narrow and Wide

Daichi tripped the gnome Narrow and told him: "If you don't want Wide to die you're better off surrendering now."

He tied Narrow up, took every weapon, bomb, elixir and alchemist's tool from both gnomes, and told them they would be judged later.

### The Chamber of Scribing

- Opened the door to the Chamber of Scribing.
- With Tal'lysae's aid, disabled the first lightning rune table, and later the northern table.
