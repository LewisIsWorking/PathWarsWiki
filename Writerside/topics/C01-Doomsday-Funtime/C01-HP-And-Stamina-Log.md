Every change to a player character's Hit Points (HP) and Stamina Points (SP), rises and falls alike, recorded by Tongs Browser in the GM's Foundry and kept by the ComeOnOverUno server. Times are UTC. A "?" is a value Tongs did not know, because it first saw the character mid-change. "manual change" means no damage or healing card came with it, such as an edit to the sheet.
