Golden rules

  1. Baybarron: say SKIP to the banish unless you've picked a target
  2. Madness needs a spare card to discard. Keep one
  3. Madness needs 2 monsters with different Types AND Attributes. Baybarron + Virakam are both Illusion: no

  5. Oceanus goes in the Extra Monster Zone
  6. Read both GYs + banishment before Invocation. Invocation can use a FIRE/WIND (or any) monster from EITHER GY: use that before spending a card from hand
