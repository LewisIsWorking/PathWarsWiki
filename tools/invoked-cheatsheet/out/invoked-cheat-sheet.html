Golden rules

  1. Baybarron: say SKIP to the banish unless you've picked a target
  2. Madness needs a spare card to discard. Keep one
  3. Oceanus goes in the Extra Monster Zone
  4. Before any Link: is there a free zone?
  5. Read both GYs + banishment before Invocation. Invocation can use a FIRE/WIND (or any) monster from EITHER GY: use that before spending a card from hand
  6. Discard order: dead copies (2nd Meltdown, used Invoke Sword) → Fuwalos → Imperm → Crossout → Ghost Belle. KEEP Maxx C, Ash, Super Poly, Droll, Nibiru
  7. Mechaba: keep 1 monster + 1 Spell/Trap in hand if you can
  8. Imperm on Aleister or Crowley: Aleister keeps its name (Invocation/Grail still work); Crowley does not

Discard order (Madness, Mechaba, Super Poly, Spenta)

  1. Dead copies: 2nd Meltdown, an Invoke Sword when another is set, Sacred Sword after you've used one
  2. Mulcharmy Fuwalos (dead once you control cards)
  3. Infinite Impermanence, unless you'll set it
  4. Crossout Designator
  5. Ghost Belle

Keep: Maxx "C", Ash, Super Poly, Droll, Nibiru, Ghost Ogre

No starter: pass with hand traps

Going 1st · brick hand · card text

No Aleister, Reminiscent, Meltdown, Aiwass, Sacred Sword, Spenta, Crowley or Zoroa

Your turn

  1. Don't summon or set anything. Fuwalos and Imperm from hand only work while you control no cards
  2. End your turn

Her turn

  1. Fuwalos: early in her Main Phase. Draw 1 for each Deck/Extra Deck summon, to find a starter
  2. Maxx "C": her first Special Summon. Ash / Droll / Imperm / Ghost Belle / Ghost Ogre: see Line S1
  3. Nibiru: after her 5th summon this turn, tributes her whole board
  4. Fuwalos and Droll don't mix: Droll blocks your Fuwalos draws

Your next turn

  1. Play the best line your new hand fits
  2. TTT: live if she activated a monster effect during your turn (e.g. a hand trap on you)
End boardNothing on board, but a full hand of interaction
RememberDon't Normal Summon Fuwalos or Nibiru: they're worth far more in hand.
